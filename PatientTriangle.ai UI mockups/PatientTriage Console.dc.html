Eight screens, one rule: the machine recommends, the nurse decides

A team spec, not a pitch reel. Every screen below is drawn in the Industry system — hairline blueprint frames, steel accent, condensed headings. Acuity is encoded by fill weight on the accent ramp (ESI 1 solid, ESI 5 outline), and one clay tone is reserved system-wide for a single meaning: an undertriage mismatch. Nothing else in the product is allowed to use it.

Text branch · what the complaint contributed

The words carried risk

“Just feeling weak and a bit feverish, off for a couple of days.”
weak→ non-specific malaise · a top-quartile term in confirmed sepsis presentations
feverish→ subjective fever, corroborated by 38.1 °C
two days→ subacute onset, raises infection over cardiac
No red-flag idiom present. This is exactly the case where a phrase-matching rule stays quiet and the fused model does not. The complaint alone reads benign; combined with the shock index it does not.
The narration layer may only reference drivers listed on this screen. It cannot introduce a claim of its own.
Counterfactual

What would drop this flag

  • SBP above 124 (shock index < 0.85)
  • RR at or below 18
  • Lactate drawn and under 2.0 mmol/L
Confidence

Moderate. Two inputs are missing and the model says so rather than smoothing over it. The uncertainty gate is what turned a borderline score into a flag.
